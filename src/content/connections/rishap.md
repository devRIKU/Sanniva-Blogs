Oh, Rishap; he’s that one friend who’s always better than you at something and will definitely boast about it. But to be honest, I like it; it’s genuinely fun being around him, if you enjoy being patronized. His knowledge on certain topics is far greater than mine, which he clearly loves reminding me of. No offense, but sometimes he tries to ignore me, a real maturity milestone for someone so "intelligent", and those are the moments when I get really sad.

And speaking of his face, it’s evident that he knows too much, as his brain is quite big (no offense), almost as big as his ego. That’s why I lovingly call him Mr. Triangle or Dorito, just to mess with him; though I’m sure he thinks it’s because he’s actually interesting. He’s always trying to outdo me in academics, but the subject he mostly manages to beat me in is math. Yeah, math—the one subject that most people mysteriously despise fitting that he’d excel at something so soulless. I get the hate, but lowkey, I kinda vibe with it, at least I don't make it my entire personality.

Now, his physique? Exceptionally slender; how convenient for him. If you’re trying to picture how thin he is, think of a needle, but even that’s too generous; he’s closer to the width of a hair strand, hardly a surprise given how little substance he has.

Because of all this, people have this weird love-hate thing going on with him. They roast him, they admire him, they roll their eyes, and yet, they still hang out with him... gluttons for punishment, I suppose. And me? Well, despite his clingy tendencies, which I’m sure he thinks are charming—he’s a good friend. Annoying sometimes (a lot), but good.

Speaking of ignoring which he wont stop doing... as if that’s a personality trait... lately he even try to sabotage others into ignoring me too! Also he is kind of clingy, but I suppose he needs the company since everyone else is just tolerating him.
