Oh Rishap—where do I even begin? First off, the guy goes feral if he scores even 0.1% less than full marks. He’ll straight-up argue with the principal just to claw his way to a perfect score. Honestly, I wouldn’t be shocked if teachers were secretly required to grade his papers three times over, just to avoid the wrath of Mr. Perfection.

And speaking of “Mr.”—his face? Shaped like an inverted triangle 🔽. That’s why I lovingly call him Mr. Triangle or Dorito, just to mess with him. He’s always trying to outdo me in academics, but the only subject he actually manages to beat me in is math. Yeah, math—the one subject that most people mysteriously despise. I get the hate, but lowkey, I kinda vibe with it.

Now, his physique? Exceptionally slender. If you’re trying to picture how thin he is, think of a needle—but even that’s too generous. He’s closer to the width of a hair strand.

Because of all this,

people have this weird love-hate thing going on with him. They roast him, they admire him, they roll their eyes—and yet, they still hang out with him. And me? Well, despite his clingy tendencies, he’s a good friend. Annoying sometimes, but good.
