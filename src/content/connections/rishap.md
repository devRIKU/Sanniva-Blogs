Oh, Rishap. He’s that one friend who’s always better than you at something and will definitely boast about it. But to be honest, I like it; it’s genuinely fun being around him. His knowledge on certain topics is far greater than mine. No offense, but sometimes he tries to ignore me, and those are the moments when I get really sad.

And speaking of his face, it’s evident that he knows too much, as his brain is quite big (no offense). That’s why I lovingly call him Mr. Triangle or Dorito, just to mess with him. He’s always trying to outdo me in academics, but the subject he mostly manages to beat me in is math. Yeah, math—the one subject that most people mysteriously despise. I get the hate, but lowkey, I kinda vibe with it.

Now, his physique? Exceptionally slender. If you’re trying to picture how thin he is, think of a needle, but even that’s too generous; he’s closer to the width of a hair strand.

Because of all this, people have this weird love-hate thing going on with him. They roast him, they admire him, they roll their eyes—and yet, they still hang out with him. And me? Well, despite his clingy tendencies, he’s a good friend. Annoying sometimes, but good.
