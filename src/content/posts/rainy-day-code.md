It’s been raining cats and dogs here since morning. School was canceled which is literally the best news ever! Since there’s no way I can go out to play or hang out, I set up a cozy coding spot right next to my bedroom window.

I made myself a mug of hot cocoa (with way too many marshmallows) and put on a 1-hour lo-fi chill tech music compilation in the background. Honestly, writing code on a rainy day has to be one of the most comforting feelings ever. 

I’m working on a simple math tutor chatbot that quizzes you on pre-algebra formulas. It’s pretty funny because if you get the answer wrong three times, the bot starts telling you terrible math puns like: `"Why was the equal sign so humble? Because it knew it wasn't less than or greater than anyone else!"`

Coding is so cool because you can build these tiny worlds out of nothing but text. It's like writing a book, but your book can answer back and play games with you! Hope everyone is having a dry and relaxing weekend. Check back soon for more updates!
