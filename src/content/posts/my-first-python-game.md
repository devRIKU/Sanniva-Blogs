So, my elder brother (who is basically my role model for anything computer-related) challenge me to write a game in Python. I thought, how hard can it be? Boy, was I wrong. 

I decided to make a text-based fantasy adventure game where you explore a school (which is secretly run by wizards). You have to find keys to open the computer lab. It sounded so easy, but then I met my worst enemy: **IndentationErrors**. Python is so picky! If my spacing is off by just one pixel (or one space, same thing), the whole screen turns red with errors.

Anyway, after about three days and drinking way too much carbonated orange soda, I finally got it to work. It’s got battle mechanics, inventory tracking, and even an Easter egg where you can challenge the principal to a game of rock-paper-scissors! My brother tried it and actually smiled (a rare event, let me tell you). 

If you want to try it, let me know. Who knows, maybe someday I’ll make a 3D version on Unity!
